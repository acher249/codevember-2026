The veiled figure is reconstructed from a photograph, "Veiled abstract figure" on StockCake (https://stockcake.com, free to use): its geometry read by MoGe-3 ViT-G (Microsoft, MIT licence; https://github.com/microsoft/MoGe), made whole and simulated in Blender.
The seated body under the cloth is from Blender Studio's Human Base Meshes (CC0).
Everything else (the statues' marble, glass and obsidian, the floor, the sky and its out-of-focus lights) is shaded in code.

Used by the development-only takes (not the one that goes out): CC0 textures from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com). No credit is required, but it is due.
